We are **Clark and Angie Wimberly**, and Superfun Games is our family game shop. We live in Jonestown, a small town on the edge of Lake Travis about thirty minutes north of Austin, Texas.

We have been making games together for years. Card games, party games, puzzles, and now daily games for the web. The throughline has always been the same: we like games that get people talking, and we are allergic to rulebooks that need a rulebook.

::: quote cite="Our entire design philosophy, roughly"
If you have to explain it twice, we cut it.
:::

## What we have made

Our first published game was [Yes, Chef!](/yes-chef/), a cooking card game where you fight to collect ingredients and match recipes in a frantic kitchen battle. It taught us a lot about how quickly a table full of strangers can turn into a table full of friends.

Then came [LeRoy and Lewis](/leroy-and-lewis-the-game/), made with the Austin barbecue joint of the same name. Working with a real brand on a real game was the moment the whitelabel idea clicked for us.

## Why daily games

Somewhere along the way we noticed that the games we could not stop playing were the small ones. The five-minute ones. The ones with a fresh puzzle every morning and a streak you did not want to break.

That is the shape we build in now, and it turns out newsrooms want exactly that: something quick, something branded, something readers come back for every single day.
