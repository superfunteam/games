## about
Texas Two Step is a **complete, ready-to-play daily word game** your readers will come back for every day. We build it, brand it, and hand it over: your logo, your colors, your domain. No dev team required.

Deceptively easy gameplay: drag the correct word tiles into the grid to complete all clues in all four directions. Flip, swap, and flop your way to glory.

Quick to learn, hard to put down, and engineered to build the daily habit that keeps an audience coming back to *your* site.
